# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, give constructive
technical feedback, and keep collaboration focused on improving the project.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- Using inclusive, professional language.
- Being clear about assumptions and limitations.
- Accepting constructive feedback and offering it with context.
- Respecting privacy, credentials, and coursework integrity.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing private
information, or knowingly adding secrets and credentials to the repository.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict
participation when behavior is harmful to the project or its contributors.
Report concerns privately to the repository owner.
